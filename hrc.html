Who We Are

Building the Future of
Humanoid Robotics

I'm the co-founder and VP of the Humanoid Robot Club at Purdue, and we're the first-ever student group building a walking humanoid robot for space exploration. With 800+ members, 8 faculty advisors, and 10+ corporate partners like AMD and Autodesk, we've gotten appreciation from the entire Purdue leadership — all in just 6 months!

Our mission is to push the boundaries of what student-built systems can do, and to give Purdue students hands-on experience with cutting-edge humanoid robotics technology.
