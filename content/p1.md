## CURRENT LIFE (Purdue)

### Starting mindset
- Came to Purdue thinking I'd just enjoy life and party

### Turning point
- Met Aarav → saw the kind of stuff he was doing
- Decided to try building things myself

### Discovering building (Fall 2023)
- Joined Purdue Space Program
- Absolutely loved it — building and flying rockets at 17 felt insane
- Saw my first rocket launch
- Started CADing for the first time

### First leadership role (Dec 2023)
- Joined PSP leadership as Branding Head
- Helped lead ~1800 students across 8 competition teams

**What I learned:**
- How to build a brand and communicate purpose
- How important marketing actually is (even if engineers don't value it)
- Growth of the org came directly from strong branding → learned how to explain that

### 2024 — Big shift (HRC begins)
- April 2024: Became VP/Secretary of PSP (freshman year)
- Around the same time, Feb 2024:
  - Aarav and I were watching Atlas robot parkour videos in a lab
  - Said we want to build this → idea for HRC
- Started:
  - Meeting professors + department heads
  - Building a team and structure
  - Eventually had to leave PSP to focus on HRC

### 2024–2025 — Building HRC
- Raised $250K+ in sponsorships (AMD, Mouser, Autodesk, etc.)
- Built first humanoid system — legs that could float mid-air (Nemo)
- Hosted "Humanoids in Action" — brought Unitree & Ameca to Purdue

### 2025 — Hackathons → SF
- Attended first hackathon → built hallof.tech
  - Interactive museum where you can talk to people like Steve Jobs, Elon Musk, Mark Zuckerberg
- This led to:
  - Getting noticed → offered internship by Prady
  - Connection to Ruslan (f.inc) → entry into SF startup ecosystem

### Summer (SF phase)
- Went to San Francisco
- Learned a lot about GTM, startups, leadership
- Mentorship from Prady + exposure to high-level talent

**Takeaways:**
- Realized how strong the competition is
- Understood how much I need to improve
- Met incredible people (but didn't love SF as a city)

### Leadership shift
- Aarav dropped out → I became President of HRC
- Learned how to actually lead a team, not just build

### 2025–2026 — StarkHacks + scaling
- Went to CalHacks & HackPrinceton → wanted to host a hardware hackathon
- Purdue set a GWR (Rubik's cube) → wanted to do something similar
- Combined both → started StarkHacks

**Execution:**
- Worked the hardest I ever have
- Should've built a bigger team — was doing too much myself

**Outcome:**
- Event went well overall — people were happy
- Some backlash (travel reimbursements), even though it was never guaranteed

### Where HRC stands now
- Built humanoid arms
- Legs took first steps on the ground

**But honestly:**
- It should've walked fully by end of year
- I know I could've done more
