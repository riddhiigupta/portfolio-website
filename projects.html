Walking Robot Dog

Building a lightweight robot dog with 4 degrees of freedom per leg. Each leg uses servo motors with inverse kinematics to achieve smooth, stable gaits. The platform is designed to be low-cost and modular for future research.

Technologies used in this build:

Arduino Servo Motors IMU Sensors 4-DoF per Leg Inverse Kinematics Custom PCB 3D Printed Parts
